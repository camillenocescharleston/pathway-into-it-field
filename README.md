## Who I Am

My name is **Camille Noces Charleston** — also known as Kolibri.

I'm a 40-year-old Haitian man living in **Fall River, MA**, working as a Security Officer while building my career in IT from the ground up. I'm multilingual (English, French, Haitian Creole, Portuguese), certified in **CompTIA CySA+** and **A+ Core 1**, and currently enrolled in Red Hat's **RH124** course at Bunker Hill Community College.

I didn't come from a traditional tech background. I came from physical security, customer service, and years of self-teaching through YouTube and free resources. What I bring that most entry-level candidates don't is this: **I understand access control, audit trails, incident documentation, and the cost of a security failure** — not from a textbook, but from years of living it on the job.

This repository is my public record of turning that foundation into an IT career.
